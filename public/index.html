What counts as aura

Aura farming is looking effortlessly cool on purpose. The meme is a boy dancing on the bow of a racing boat at the Pacu Jalur races in Riau, Indonesia: black outfit, sunglasses, a stone-faced calm and slow, smooth moves.

  • Calm face. No grinning, no laughing.
  • Chin up, gaze steady on the horizon.
  • Slow, smooth moves. Sweep a hand forward, roll your fists like a wheel, hold one arm forward and one back.
  • Sunglasses help. Trying too hard doesn't.

How it scores

Every 1.25 s, two frames half a second apart go to Microsoft-Decision-1.1 with three questions: is this aura farming, how much aura, and which move. Aura found pays up to +1000 a check, for Legendary.

Frames go to this app's server and its Microsoft Foundry deployment for the check, then they are dropped. Nothing is saved.
